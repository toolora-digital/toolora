Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use Toolora.

Last updated: October 3, 2026
01

Introduction

Welcome to Toolora. Toolora provides free online calculators, productivity tools, student utilities, games, and other digital resources.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you visit or use Toolora, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

02

Information We Collect

Toolora is designed to provide many tools without requiring you to create an account.

Depending on how you use the website, information may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us, such as your name and email address.
  • Information entered into a tool or calculator while using that tool.
  • Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, and referring pages where provided through standard web services.
  • Usage information that may help us understand how visitors interact with the website.

We do not require personal information simply to use most Toolora calculators and tools.

03

Information Entered Into Tools

Many Toolora tools perform calculations or generate results directly in your browser.

Information entered into a calculator or utility may be processed locally by the tool. Toolora does not automatically require you to submit that information to us.

However, third-party services or future features may operate differently. Where applicable, additional information will be provided on the relevant page.

04

How Information May Be Used

Information available to us may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and maintaining Toolora.
  • Improving the performance, usability, and content of our website.
  • Understanding general website usage and traffic.
  • Responding to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Detecting technical problems, abuse, or security issues.
05

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Toolora or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies can be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, understanding website traffic, improving functionality, or supporting advertising services.

You can usually control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

06

Advertising

Toolora may display advertising from third-party advertising providers.

These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements according to their own policies and applicable settings.

If advertising services are added or changed, relevant third-party privacy practices may apply alongside this policy.

07

Third-Party Services

Toolora may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, advertising, forms, or other website functionality.

These services may process certain information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Toolora does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information handled through Toolora.

However, no website, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Children's Privacy

Toolora is intended to provide general-purpose online utilities and does not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us unnecessarily, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10

External Links

Toolora may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of websites that we do not operate.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

11

Your Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is used.

You may also control certain browser-level technologies such as cookies through your device or browser settings.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Toolora, our services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date.

13

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Toolora handles information, you can contact us through our Contact page.
