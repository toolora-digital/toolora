How it works

Understand your profit

Toolora calculates your profit by subtracting the cost price from the selling price. It then calculates your profit margin based on the selling price and also shows your markup based on the cost price.

Formulas

Profit, margin and markup

These calculations help you understand how much profit you make from each sale.

Profit
Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin
Profit ÷ Selling Price × 100
Markup
Profit ÷ Cost Price × 100
